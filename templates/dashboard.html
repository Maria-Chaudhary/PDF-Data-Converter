    How your PDFs are handled

    • Temporary processing only. An uploaded PDF is written to a temporary folder while it is being processed and deleted as soon as the request finishes.
    • Nothing is stored. No PDF, extracted text or invoice data is saved in a database or kept on the server.
    • OCR runs on the server. Scanned pages are read with Tesseract, so your documents are not sent to any third-party service.
    • Results live in your browser. Extracted data stays on this page until you download it or leave. Recent conversions list file names and counts only, and are saved in this browser's local storage. Clear them any time.
